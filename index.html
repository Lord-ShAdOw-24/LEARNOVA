About Learnova

Built in Cameroon, for Cameroonian learners

Learnova was founded to give every O'Level, A'Level and Concours candidate in the Anglophone sub-system a fair shot at excellent revision material, honest ranking and trustworthy live teaching — whether they study in Bamenda, Buea, Douala or a village school with one shared smartphone.
